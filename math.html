Simple Calculator Math

This page explains the baseline calculation without changing or rerunning a property analysis. It does not make a RentCast request.

1. Property data

The Simple Calculator makes one RentCast valuation request. That response supplies the subject-property information, RentCast valuation/range, and comparable properties used by the calculator.

2. Simple ARV

The Simple result is the baseline ARV stored from the initial analysis. It is intentionally kept separate from any changes you test in Advanced.

Simple ARV = baseline comp-supported valuation returned by the analysis

3. Rehab estimate

If you leave the manual rehab field blank, the calculator estimates rehab from the subject's finished square footage and your selected renovation level. A manual rehab amount overrides the automatic estimate.

Automatic rehab = subject square feet × selected rehab $/sq ft

4. Total basis

Total basis = purchase price + rehab + other costs

5. Refinance

Estimated refinance = Simple ARV × selected LTV

6. Cash result

Cash result = estimated refinance − total basis

A positive number means estimated refinance proceeds exceed the modeled basis. A negative number represents estimated cash remaining in the project.

7. Why Advanced is separate

Advanced starts from the frozen Simple ARV, then uses the already-returned comp set to estimate how proposed bedroom, bathroom, and finished-area changes could affect value. Those experiments never rewrite the Simple baseline.

These are screening estimates, not a lender appraisal or a guarantee of refinance proceeds.
